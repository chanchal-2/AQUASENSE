About AquaSense

AI-Driven Water Security for Bengaluru

AquaSense is an intelligent early-warning system designed to tackle urban water scarcity. By integrating multi-source environmental data with predictive machine learning, we provide actionable insights to both city administrators and everyday citizens.

Our Mission

To eliminate preventable water shortages through predictive analytics, enabling proactive resource management across all 198 BBMP wards of Bengaluru.

The Problem

Bengaluru faces severe groundwater depletion, erratic monsoons, and rising demand. Traditional monitoring is reactive — AquaSense makes it predictive.

Our Approach

We compute a Water Stress Index (WSI) per ward using rainfall, borewell depth, reservoir levels, population density, and BWSSB supply data — all processed by ML.

Key Features

Everything You Need to Stay Ahead

Interactive Ward Map

Color-coded risk visualization across all wards with real-time WSI scoring.

AI Forecasting

30 & 60-day predictions using Random Forest Regressor trained on historical data.

Smart Alerts

Automated critical notifications when wards cross WSI danger thresholds.
